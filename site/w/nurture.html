nurture

動詞・名詞/ˈnɝtʃɚ/CEFR B2ターゲット1900 #1210
  1. 【動詞】（…を）育てる、養う（to provide with food, care, and protection；to help someone or something grow and develop）
  2. 【動詞】（感情・能力などを）育む、培う、促進する（to foster or encourage the development or growth of a feeling, ability, or talent）
  3. 【名詞】育成、教育、養育（the care, attention, training, and education given to help someone develop）
