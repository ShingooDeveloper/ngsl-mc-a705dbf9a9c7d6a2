tower

名詞・動詞/ˈtaʊɚ/CEFR B1頻度#2321
  1. 【名詞】tall narrow building or structure（watchtower等）
  2. 【名詞】structure for defense or observation（guard tower等）
  3. 【動詞】to rise high or stand much taller than something
