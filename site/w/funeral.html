funeral

名詞/ˈfjuːnɚəl/CEFR B1ターゲット1400 #1360
  1. 【名詞】葬儀、葬式、埋葬式（…a ceremony to mourn and remember someone who has died…）
  2. 【名詞】（比喩・俗語）それはおしまいだ、終わりだ（…something that is finished or ruined…）
