resort

名詞・動詞/ˈriːzɔrt/ (noun); /rɪˈzɔrt/ (verb)CEFR B2頻度#2255
  1. 【名詞】保養地・リゾート (a place or facility designed for vacation, rest, and recreation)
  2. 【名詞】最終手段・最後の手段 (a final option chosen when no other alternatives remain)
  3. 【動詞】（…に）頼る・すがる (to turn to something as a means or solution when other options fail)
