slim

形容詞・動詞/slɪm/CEFR B1ターゲット1200 #888
  1. 【形容詞】(細身の、スリムな) thin and of a pleasing shape; having a graceful slender figure
  2. 【形容詞】(わずかな、小さな) small in size or likelihood; a slim chance of success
  3. 【動詞】(スリムにする、細くする) to make or become slim or thinner; to reduce in size
