wisdom_tooth

名詞/ˈwɪz.dəm ˈtuːθ/CEFR B1英検準1級 #2711
  1. 【名詞】親知らず。成人期に奥歯の後ろに生える大臼歯。（one of four large teeth that grow at the back of your mouth in late adolescence or early adulthood）
  2. 【名詞】（比喩的に）思慮・判断力。良い判断や分別のこと。（wisdom or good judgment in making decisions）
